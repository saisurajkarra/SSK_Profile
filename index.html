02 · Labs

Three models you can break

Not screenshots. Working models of the problems behind these projects. Drag the power market, reject the agent's plan, change the physics, then copy a link to your exact scenario.

An air separation plant makes liquid oxygen and nitrogen around the clock, but electricity is not priced around the clock. Store product in a tank and you can make more when power is cheap and less when it is not. The optimizer below finds that schedule; you decide which changes to approve.

Loading the lab…

Air is separated by distillation: stack enough equilibrium stages and push enough liquid back down, and nitrogen leaves the top. How many stages, and how much reflux? Step through a McCabe–Thiele column and watch the capital-versus-energy trade-off appear.

A landfill keeps making methane for decades after it closes. The EPA's first-order-decay method turns tonnage, climate and capture rate into a 60-year gas curve, which is the raw material for renewable natural gas projects.
